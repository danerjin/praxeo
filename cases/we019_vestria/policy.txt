Vestria Fleet Renewal Rebate (fictional; summary of the policy record of handbook worked example WE-019).

Vestria is a country of about 9 million people with no domestic car manufacturing: new cars are imported by about 30 brand importers and sold through some 600 franchised dealers. About 200,000 new cars are sold in a normal year, roughly 40% of them models that use 5.0 liters of fuel per 100 km or less. Of 4.5 million registered cars, about 1.1 million are 12 or more years old; a drivable car of that age typically sells for V$1,500 to V$5,000. Used cars can be imported, but they pay a 25% duty and a registration inspection, so the used-car market runs mostly on the domestic fleet. Neighboring countries have no scheme.

The government announced the rebate in its budget statement on 15 March. Parliament enacted it on 20 May. It applies to purchase contracts signed from 1 July to 31 December of the same year and then expires. Ministers have said there is no plan to repeat it, but have not ruled it out.

A private buyer who scraps an eligible old car receives a rebate of V$4,000 on a new car certified at 5.0 L/100 km or less, bought from a registered Vestrian dealer. The rebate is paid by the Transport Ministry to the dealer and deducted on the invoice; one per buyer. The old car must be 12 or more years old, registered to the buyer continuously since before 15 March, and hold a current roadworthiness certificate. The budget is capped at V$480 million (120,000 rebates), first come, first served; the scheme closes early if the budget runs out. Business and rental fleets and cars bought for export are excluded.

The old car is delivered to a licensed dismantler, deregistered, and crushed within 30 days. Only scrap metal may be sold; no parts may be resold. Dismantlers receive a V$150 handling fee per car.

The rebate, the fees and the administration are funded by additional government bonds issued in the year, serviced and repaid from general revenue over 10 years.

The Transport Ministry vehicle registry checks registration history and roadworthiness certificates, confirms deregistration and audits dismantlers. False claims are subject to clawback of the rebate and fines.
