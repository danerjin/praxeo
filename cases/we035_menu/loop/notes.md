## What the format could not hold
1. A disclosure's effect depends on the sign of each buyer's belief error. `terms` carries one direction per exchange, so I split the buyer side into two exchanges (two subclasses) in the seed; the answer is then the dominance fact on A-4, which is proposable. Item-level mix (which items are bought) has no variable: only quantity_traded of meals, so "away from heavy items" shows up as trades lost at the chain, which also needs A-21 to hold.
2. The sign of an `alternatives` fact is the sign of the use of the alternative, so reformulating to lighter items can only be measured by `product_attribute` increase, which reads as "calories up". It stands for "extent of reformulation"; a variable for the calorie content itself with its own sign is not expressible.
3. A fixed cost per item (`basis: item`) gives `variety` decrease with no slot for its premise (A-6, the per-item cost is material for the lowest-volume items): `quantity_premises` only gates the trade count, not the variety fall, and `branch_premises` is for a choice of branch. The variety fall is therefore signed without A-6. The same holds for the operating (per-outlet) charge and A-24: number_of_sellers decrease is unconditional; only the price rise is gated by A-24 through `moves`.
4. `terms` and `moves` are seed-only (not in the proposable parts). The silence inference (A-16: buyers read an exempt restaurant's silence as bad, CR-11 silence) needs a `terms` fact on e2, which I did not seed and the loop cannot add; neither can A-23 (exempt restaurants posting voluntarily so that L2 applies to them), because sellers.exempt is not a chooser under any component and so cannot take an alternative. Class responses to losing buyers (adopting the rival practice) are not expressible for a class the policy does not bind.
5. The cap on fines ($10,000 per inspection; `capped_payers`) needs a payer class; the text does not say which chains reach it, so it is not seeded. Fines are also a risk (penalty attached to non-compliance), not a payment everyone pays: modelled as a branch charge with A-17, and the cap and the two fine amounts are lost.
6. Receipts of private suppliers (labs, board makers) have no receipt variable in the list; I used `earnings`. `number_of_sellers` stands for outlets, and also for the measure of a chain's size at the threshold (no variable for outlets per chain).
7. Not used (no slot that fits): A-8 (reputation cost, deters misreporting), A-20 (serving variation and portion control), A-22 (buyers who look counts up on websites: already in a1's second half), A-2 (counts within tolerance), A-14 (boards replaced anyway: lowers the net board cost; no way to state a cost net of replacement that would have happened), A-13 beyond the branch. A-6, A-3 and A-1 are used only as stated above. The announced fact has no effect because the timing options (bringing boards or items forward) are not in the text.
8. A link between two variables of one class with a reading (reclassification -> variety under U1-b) works, but the per-reading signs are only shown collapsed in the final list ("ambiguous" for reclassification and variety).
