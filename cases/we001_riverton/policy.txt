Riverton Rent Stabilization Ordinance (fictional; handbook worked example WE-001).

Rent on residential rental units existing on the effective date is frozen at its level on the effective date for 3 years; increases are capped at 2% a year after that.

The cap applies to the unit, including when a new tenant moves in.

Units first occupied after the effective date are exempt.

Eviction only for cause. Tenancies cannot be assigned or sold.

Conversion of covered buildings to owner-occupied condominiums is permitted after a 12-month permit process; sitting tenants must be given notice and relocation payments.

A rent board enforces the ordinance on complaint; overcharging is fined; the board is funded from the city's general budget.
