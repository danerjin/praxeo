## Reading

Eight of 71 rows are produced (two in part) and one engine row is WRONG. The congestion charge is the most distant example from WE-013: most of its table is about road space (a shared facility, CR-9, Tier C), substitution among modes, and the earmark's bus service, none of which the tax and tariff rules touch. The rows that are produced come from the generic chains (TX-1, TX-2, TX-9, OC-3, OC-4, RS-5, RS-14).

The WRONG row is an artefact of a default: a record whose good has no private seller still gets `sellers.current`, and OC-4 then signs a loss of exchange for it. The fix is a record fact (the good has no private seller), not a new pattern.

RS-14 transfers well: the carriers' charge reaches the businesses they deliver to (C-46) with the same rule that passes steel to builders in WE-013.
