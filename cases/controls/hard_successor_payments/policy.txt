Jessop Rent Cap (fictional control case). Yearly rent increases for sitting tenants are capped at 1 percent. A tenancy passes, at the capped rent, to any person the tenant names in writing as successor, who takes over the tenancy on the same terms. In Jessop, outgoing tenants commonly receive a payment from the person they name. Market rents rise about 6 percent a year, and the Rent Board enforces the cap.
