Dunmore Rent Cap (fictional control case). Yearly rent increases for sitting tenants are capped at 1 percent, and the rent stays capped when a new tenant moves in. A Rent Board with 120 inspectors audits every registered unit each year and orders refunds plus fines of three times any overcharge. Market rents in Dunmore have risen about 8 percent a year for a decade, and the vacancy rate is 1 percent. Tenancies cannot be assigned or sublet.
