Ashby Rent Ordinance (fictional control case). The ordinance caps the rent of every rental unit at 200 percent of the city's median rent on 1 January 2030. Market rents in Ashby have fallen for three years in a row and the vacancy rate is 9 percent. No unit in the city currently rents for more than 140 percent of the median. The Rent Board enforces the cap and may fine landlords who exceed it.
