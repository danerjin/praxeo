Lakeport Beverage Tax (FICTIONAL: Lakeport and the tax are invented for handbook worked example WE-002 and resemble, but are not, any real city drink tax).

Lakeport is a town of about 150,000 people. Three neighboring towns, each 10 to 15 minutes' drive from most Lakeport homes, have no such tax. Many Lakeport households already do part of their grocery shopping in large stores just outside the town line.

Distributors must pay an excise tax of 1.5 cents per fluid ounce on drinks with added sugar that they deliver to retailers in Lakeport. The tax is collected from distributors on deliveries into the town. Drinks sweetened only with non-caloric sweeteners, milk-based drinks and 100% juice are exempt.

The revenue funds free pre-school places for 3- and 4-year-olds, run by the town and by approved providers. The town's revenue department administers the tax, with distributor registration, monthly returns and audits, funded from the tax's own revenue.

It is unclear whether approved providers may include existing private pre-schools on equal terms. One reading is yes, with limits on places (the reading assumed in the text); the other is no.
