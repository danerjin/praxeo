## Reading

Eight of 68 rows are produced (two in part) and one engine row is WRONG. As with WE-046, the table is mostly about markets the tax rules do not reach: the recycled-resin market, substitutes, the exemption notch, investment and asset values. The generic tax chains give what they give for the other examples, and RS-14 (packaging users' costs passed to the buyers of packaged goods) transfers.

The WRONG row is a gap in OC-3: it names the taxpayers as payers of the new administration, which is right where the budget is raised from them and wrong where the tax's own revenue pays (the handbook's third funding case, which WE-002, WE-046 and WE-062 all state). The question `enforcement_budget_raised` cannot tell the cases apart, and the honest answer (A-24: an appropriation from the general budget) yields the wrong sign.
